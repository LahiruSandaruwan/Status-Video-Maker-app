# StatusPro - Marketing Strategy & Plan

## 🎯 Target Audience

### Demographics
- **Age**: 16-35 years old
- **Gender**: All genders (slight female majority for love/celebration content)
- **Location**: Sri Lanka, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Southeast Asia
- **Income**: Middle to lower-middle class
- **Device**: Android-heavy market (90%+)

### Psychographics
- **Behavior**: Active on social media, especially WhatsApp and Instagram
- **Interests**: Creating and sharing status videos, expressing emotions, staying trendy
- **Pain Points**:
  - Difficulty creating professional-looking videos
  - Limited editing skills
  - Time-consuming video creation
  - Expensive professional tools

3. **Birthday & Celebrations** (20%): Birthday wishes, achievement celebrations
4. **Lyrics Videos** (15%): Music lovers, song enthusiasts
5. **Photo Slideshows** (5%): Memories, travel, events

## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

### Potential Risks
1. **High Competition**: Many video maker apps exist
   - **Solution**: Focus on speed (2-min creation) and quality templates

2. **Ad Fatigue**: Users annoyed by ads
   - **Solution**: Balanced ad placement, rewarded ads provide value

3. **Copyright Issues**: Music/content copyright
   - **Solution**: Use only royalty-free music, clear licensing

4. **Technical Issues**: App crashes, bugs
   - **Solution**: Extensive testing, quick bug fixes, monitoring

5. **Market Saturation**: Hard to stand out
   - **Solution**: Focus on underserved markets (Sri Lanka), local language support
